Common questions

How do I make an invoice?
Tap New invoice. Everything is on one page: tap the days you worked, enter your hours and rate, choose a client and add your payment details, then tap Create invoice at the bottom. Your invoice is saved and ready to send.

Can I finish an invoice later?
Yes. Close it with ✕ and choose Keep as draft. It waits at the top of your invoices on the home screen, marked Draft — tap it to carry on.

One day had different hours or pay. Can I change just that day?
Yes. Under Pay, tap Each date and change the hours or rate for that date. Leave a box blank to use the normal hours and rate.

Create invoice says something is missing.
Every invoice needs at least one date, hours and rate, a client, and your payment details (account holder, 6-digit sort code, 8-digit account number). Tap Create invoice and the app tells you what's missing and takes you to it.

How do I send it?
After Create invoice, tap Send invoice: Mail opens with your client's email address, a short message and the PDF attached — you press Send. Or tap Share (Messages, WhatsApp, Files…) or Print. Nothing is ever sent without you.

How do I mark an invoice as paid?
On the home screen tap Unpaid next to the invoice (or open it and tap Mark as paid), then pick the date and how you were paid. Changed your mind? Open it and tap ⋯ → Mark unpaid.

Can I say my invoice instead of typing it?
Yes, with Invoice Drop Pro. Tap Create by smart voice on the home screen and say something like "Mondays and Wednesdays next month for Greenfield Pharmacy, 8 hours at £30 an hour". Check what it heard — anything marked Check is worth a second look — then tap Use this. Pro includes up to 100 Smart voice invoices a month; the button shows how many are left and when they reset.

How do I change the look?
On the invoice page, choose a style. Classic is free; Pro adds Modern, Minimal, Bold, Elegant, Emerald, Claret and Slate. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Do I need an invoice number?
No. New invoices have no number unless you add one: tap Invoice no. on the invoice and type it.

How do I delete an invoice?
Swipe it left on the home screen, press and hold it, or open it and tap ⋯ → Delete invoice. This can't be undone.

I made a mistake on an invoice I already sent.
Open the invoice from the home screen, tap Edit, change it and send it again. The original is kept in Invoice history marked "Replaced" and isn't counted twice.

How do I see the guide again?
Tap the gear icon → Show the guide.

How do I back up my invoices?
Sign in (gear icon → Sign in) and your invoices are backed up to your account and kept on all your devices. Or tap the gear icon → Back up to Files (iCloud Drive is a good choice). A backup file includes bank details, so keep it private.

How do I move my invoices to a new iPhone?
Sign in with the same account on the new iPhone. Or back up on the old iPhone, then on the new one tap the gear icon → Restore from backup and pick the file. Restoring only adds invoices that are missing; it never deletes anything.

Can I get my invoices into a spreadsheet?
Gear icon → Export [year] as spreadsheet creates a CSV file that opens in Numbers or Excel.

How do I cancel Invoice Drop Pro?
Open the iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or in the app tap the gear icon → Manage subscription.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. If you sign in, your invoices are also stored in your account so they're backed up and on all your devices. Smart voice (Pro) sends only the words you said — never the recording — to be understood, and only after you allow it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
