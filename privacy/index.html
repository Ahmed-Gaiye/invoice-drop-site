Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Invoice Drop ("the app") is made by Pulseni. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Without an account, nothing leaves your iPhone unless you choose to share or export it. If you sign in, your invoices are backed up to your account so they're safe and appear on your other devices. We never sell your information, show ads or track you.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your own details (name or business, address, email, phone, VAT number), your clients' details, your invoices and your unfinished draft are stored on your iPhone.
  • Bank details you choose to save for future invoices are stored in your iPhone's encrypted Keychain.
  • The app has no analytics and no advertising.

Accounts (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in (with Apple, Google or an email address):

  • What's stored: your email address and an account ID; your clients, your invoices (including the bank details printed on them) and your saved payment and sender details.
  • Why: only to keep your invoices safe and in sync across your devices. Nothing is used for advertising or shared with anyone else.
  • Where: Google Firebase. Your invoices are kept in Cloud Firestore in the UK (London); sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, which may process your email address and account ID outside the UK under Google's standard safeguards. Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, only you can read your data, and Google acts as our processor under its terms.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google: we receive only your name (if you choose to share it), email address and an account ID. If you use Hide My Email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Deleting your account: ⚙️ menu → Delete account. Your account and everything backed up in it are deleted straight away, and the Sign in with Apple link is revoked. Invoices on your iPhone are kept until you delete them or the app.

Invoice Drop Pro

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether Pro is active. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Voice

When you tap the microphone and say an invoice, your speech is turned into text by Apple's built-in speech recognition. Where your iPhone supports it this happens on the iPhone itself; otherwise Apple processes it under Apple's privacy policy. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the words are then understood by Apple's on-device model, which runs entirely on your iPhone. The audio isn't stored and we never receive it. The app only listens while the microphone screen is open.

Smart voice (optional). If you're signed in and choose to use Smart voice (the app asks first), the words you said — the text, never the audio — plus the names of your saved clients and today's date are sent to our server (Google Firebase, London) and from there to our AI providers, TypeSafe (typesafe.ai) and OpenAI (openai.com), which work out the dates, client, hours and rate. They process it only to answer that request, as our processors; we don't use it for advertising and don't keep the words after answering. We keep a count of how many times you've used Smart voice, to apply monthly limits. You can say no and keep using voice on your iPhone only.

Address suggestions

When you type an address or postcode (yours or a client's), the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to suggest matching addresses. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; it isn't linked to your invoices and we never receive it. Your location is not used.

When information leaves your iPhone

Only when you choose to:

  • Signing in — your invoices are backed up to your account (see Accounts above).
  • Smart voice — only if you allow it: the words you said go to our AI providers to be understood (see Voice above).
  • Sharing an invoice PDF — it goes wherever you pick in the share sheet (for example Mail, Messages or Files).
  • Backups — a backup file contains all of your invoice data, including bank details. It is saved wherever you choose. Keep it somewhere private.
  • Spreadsheet export — a CSV of your invoices for a year, saved or shared wherever you choose.

Services you share to (such as Mail or iCloud Drive) handle that information under their own privacy policies.

Device backups

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup, as with other apps. This is managed by your device settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. If you have an account, delete it from the ⚙️ menu to remove your data from our servers too (or email us and we'll do it). Backup or spreadsheet files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete them from where you saved them.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time by emailing support@pulseni.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
